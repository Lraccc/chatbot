Yamaha Aerox Technical Specifications

Engine Specifications

Aerox 155 (Current Model)
- Engine Type: Liquid-cooled, 4-stroke, SOHC, 4-valve
- Displacement: 155cc
- Bore x Stroke: 58.0 mm x 58.7 mm
- Compression Ratio: 11.0:1
- Maximum Power: 15.4 PS @ 8,000 rpm
- Maximum Torque: 13.9 Nm @ 6,500 rpm
- Fuel System: Fuel injection
- Engine Oil Capacity: 1.0 liter
- Starting System: Electric start

Dimensions and Weight
- Overall Length: 1,960 mm
- Overall Width: 740 mm
- Overall Height: 1,145 mm
- Wheelbase: 1,350 mm
- Seat Height: 790 mm
- Ground Clearance: 135 mm
- Wet Weight: Approximately 115-120 kg (varies by version)

Fuel System
- Fuel Tank Capacity: 5.5 liters
- Fuel Type: Unleaded gasoline (RON 91 or higher recommended)
- Average Fuel Consumption: 35-45 km/l (depending on riding conditions)

Transmission
- Type: Automatic V-belt transmission (CVT)
- Final Drive: Belt drive

Chassis and Suspension
- Frame Type: Underbone frame
- Front Suspension: Telescopic fork with 110 mm travel
- Rear Suspension: Swing arm with dual shock absorbers, 95 mm travel

Braking System
- Front Brake: Single hydraulic disc brake (230 mm)
- Rear Brake: Single hydraulic disc brake (230 mm)
- ABS: Available on select models
- Brake Type: Dual-channel ABS or single-channel ABS depending on variant

Wheels and Tires
- Front Wheel: 14-inch aluminum alloy
- Rear Wheel: 13-inch aluminum alloy
- Front Tire Size: 110/80-14
- Rear Tire Size: 140/70-13
- Tire Type: Tubeless

Electrical System
- Battery: 12V, 5Ah
- Headlight: LED (on newer models)
- Tail Light: LED

Aerox 50cc (European Model)
- Engine Type: 2-stroke, liquid-cooled (older models) or 4-stroke (newer models)
- Displacement: 50cc
- Maximum Speed: Approximately 45 km/h (restricted versions)
- Power Output: Around 2-3 kW

Performance Specifications
- Top Speed (155cc): Approximately 110-120 km/h
- Acceleration 0-60 km/h: Approximately 5-6 seconds
- Fuel Economy: 35-45 km/l (under normal riding conditions)

Color Options (varies by market and year)
- Matte Red
- Matte Blue
- Racing Blue
- Black
- White
- Special edition colors and graphics packages
