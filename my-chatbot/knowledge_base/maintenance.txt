Yamaha Aerox Maintenance Guide

Regular Maintenance Schedule

Daily Checks
- Check tire pressure and condition
- Inspect lights (headlight, tail light, turn signals)
- Check brake operation
- Inspect for any fluid leaks
- Ensure mirrors are properly adjusted

Weekly Checks
- Clean the scooter body and remove dirt
- Check brake fluid level
- Inspect chain/belt for proper tension (if applicable)
- Check coolant level

Every 1,000 km or Monthly
- Check and adjust tire pressure (Front: 200 kPa / Rear: 225 kPa)
- Inspect brake pads for wear
- Clean and lubricate drive chain (if applicable)
- Check all lights and electrical systems
- Inspect throttle operation

Every 3,000 km or 3 Months
- Change engine oil
- Replace oil filter
- Check spark plug condition
- Inspect air filter and clean if necessary
- Check V-belt condition
- Inspect brake pads thickness
- Check coolant level and condition
- Lubricate cables and pivot points

Every 6,000 km or 6 Months
- Replace air filter
- Check valve clearance
- Inspect fuel lines and connections
- Clean fuel filter (if applicable)
- Check battery condition and clean terminals
- Inspect suspension for leaks or damage
- Check wheel bearings
- Replace brake fluid

Every 12,000 km or 1 Year
- Replace spark plug
- Replace V-belt (or inspect thoroughly)
- Change coolant
- Complete brake system inspection
- Inspect and clean fuel injectors
- Check exhaust system for leaks
- Inspect frame and chassis for cracks

Engine Oil Specifications
- Recommended Oil: YAMALUBE 10W-40 or equivalent
- Alternative: SAE 10W-30 or 10W-40 API grade SL or higher
- Oil Capacity: 1.0 liter (with oil filter change)
- Oil Change Interval: Every 3,000 km or 3 months

Tire Maintenance
- Front Tire Pressure: 200 kPa (29 psi)
- Rear Tire Pressure: 225 kPa (33 psi)
- Minimum Tread Depth: 1.6 mm
- Replacement: When tread wear indicators are visible

Battery Maintenance
- Type: 12V 5Ah MF (Maintenance Free)
- Check terminals for corrosion monthly
- Clean with baking soda solution if corroded
- Charge if voltage drops below 12.4V
- Expected Life: 2-3 years

Brake Maintenance
- Brake Fluid: DOT 3 or DOT 4
- Change brake fluid every 6-12 months
- Minimum Brake Pad Thickness: 1.0 mm
- Inspect brake discs for warping or scoring
- Front brake pad life: 5,000-10,000 km
- Rear brake pad life: 10,000-15,000 km

V-Belt (CVT) Maintenance
- Inspect every 3,000 km
- Replace every 12,000-15,000 km or when worn
- Signs of wear: reduced acceleration, slipping, squealing noise
- Belt width should not be less than minimum specification

Coolant System
- Coolant Type: Ethylene glycol-based coolant (50:50 mix with distilled water)
- Capacity: Approximately 0.8-1.0 liter
- Change Interval: Every 2 years or 12,000 km
- Check level when engine is cold

Common Maintenance Tasks

Oil Change Procedure
1. Warm up engine for 2-3 minutes
2. Place scooter on center stand on level ground
3. Remove oil filler cap
4. Remove drain plug and let oil drain completely
5. Replace drain plug with new washer
6. Add recommended oil (approximately 1.0 liter)
7. Check oil level with dipstick
8. Replace oil filler cap

Air Filter Cleaning
1. Remove seat and access air filter box
2. Remove air filter element
3. Clean with air compressor or tap gently to remove dust
4. If very dirty, replace with new filter
5. Reinstall filter and cover

Spark Plug Inspection
1. Remove seat to access plug
2. Disconnect spark plug cap
3. Clean area around plug
4. Remove plug with proper spark plug wrench
5. Check gap (should be 0.7-0.8 mm)
6. Look for fouling or damage
7. Replace if electrode is worn or insulator is cracked
8. Install with proper torque

Troubleshooting Common Issues

Engine Won't Start
- Check fuel level
- Ensure kill switch is in ON position
- Check battery charge
- Inspect spark plug
- Verify fuel pump operation

Poor Acceleration
- Check V-belt wear
- Clean or replace air filter
- Check spark plug condition
- Inspect fuel injector
- Check exhaust for blockage

Unusual Noises
- Rattling: Check for loose panels or components
- Squealing: Inspect V-belt condition
- Grinding: Check brake pads and discs
- Knocking: Check engine oil level

Overheating
- Check coolant level
- Inspect radiator for blockage
- Check cooling fan operation
- Ensure proper oil level
- Don't ride in extreme traffic for extended periods

Storage Tips
- Fill fuel tank and add stabilizer
- Change oil before storage
- Remove battery and store in cool, dry place
- Inflate tires to proper pressure
- Cover scooter to protect from dust
- Store in dry, ventilated area

Winter Maintenance
- Check battery more frequently
- Use appropriate oil viscosity for temperature
- Keep fuel tank full to prevent condensation
- Warm up engine longer before riding
- Check tire pressure as cold weather reduces pressure
